Autofixer Customer Policy: Repair Financing

1. About this Policy

1.1 This Policy sets out what you agree to, and what we expect of you, when you request or receive financing for a vehicle repair through Autofixer.

1.2 In this Policy, “Autofixer”, “we” and “us” mean Autofixer and any of its subsidiaries, now or in the future, that provide the Autofixer service to you. Financing is provided by a licensed lending partner of Autofixer (the “Lender”). The Lender for your request is named in the loan agreement you are offered. We may work with more than one lending partner and may add or replace them from time to time. The Lender alone decides whether to approve a request and on what terms. Autofixer is not a lender. Autofixer does not give loans, collect repayments, hold security for loans or recover vehicles.

1.3 If you are approved you will sign a loan agreement with the Lender (the “Loan Agreement”). All terms of the loan are set only by the Loan Agreement and the Lender’s other documents, and are the Lender’s responsibility. This includes the amount, interest, fees, repayment schedule and methods, late charges, early repayment, security, default, recovery and sale. This Policy does not set or change any of them. If this Policy conflicts with the Loan Agreement on any loan term, the Loan Agreement prevails.

1.4 By accepting this Policy you confirm that you are at least 18, that you can legally enter this Policy, and that everything you tell us is true and complete.

2. Key terms

  • Vehicle: the vehicle being repaired, identified by the registration number you give us.
  • Mechanic: the mechanic or workshop that carries out the repair.
  • Repair: all work, parts and services supplied for the Vehicle under your request.
  • Protected Parties: Autofixer, its affiliates, directors, officers, employees and agents, and the Lender.

3. Who does what

3.1 Autofixer provides the platform you use to submit your request and invoice, and passes your request to the Lender. Autofixer does not repair vehicles, inspect repairs or guarantee anyone’s work. It does not give loans, collect repayments or recover security.

3.2 The Lender assesses requests, funds approved repairs, holds the loan and any security, collects repayments, and deals with default and recovery, all under the Loan Agreement.

3.3 Mechanics are independent businesses. They are not employees, agents or partners of Autofixer or the Lender. An “approved” mechanic is one that has registered with Autofixer and passed Autofixer’s registration checks. Approval is not a guarantee of skill, quality, price, honesty or outcome.

3.4 You choose your mechanic and you instruct the work. You are the person best placed to check it.

3.5 Ecoswitch members and groups. If you are an Ecoswitch member linked to a group, any responsibility of group members for each other’s loans is set only by the Lender’s group agreement and the Membership Terms. Nothing in this Policy reduces your own duty to repay under your Loan Agreement.

4. Your responsibility for the Repair

4.1 You are responsible for making sure the Repair is done properly. Autofixer and the Lender do not supervise or check the work.

4.2 Before work starts, agree the following with the Mechanic in writing (a message is enough): the faults to be fixed, the work to be done, the parts to be used (new or used, and brand), the price of parts and labor, the time it will take, and any warranty.

4.3 Ask for an itemised invoice. The parts and labor costs you enter on Autofixer must match that invoice exactly.

4.4 Before you accept the Vehicle back, inspect it, take a test drive, ask to see replaced parts, and confirm that all agreed work is done and the Vehicle is safe to drive. If you are unsure, get an independent mechanic to check it.

4.5 Do not sign or confirm completion until you are satisfied. Where the Lender pays the Mechanic after you confirm completion, your confirmation means you accept the work and authorise payment.

4.6 If a defect appears later, tell the Mechanic in writing straight away, allow a reasonable chance to fix it, keep photos and records, and claim under any warranty the Mechanic gave.

4.7 Do not drive a Vehicle you believe to be unsafe.

4.8 Help us with any complaint investigation by providing messages, photos and documents when asked.

4.9 You should keep comprehensive motor insurance on the Vehicle, and meet any insurance requirement in your Loan Agreement.

5. Risk, disclaimers and indemnity

5.1 The Repair is at your risk. The Protected Parties are not responsible for: the quality, safety or completeness of any work; the quality, origin or authenticity of any part; delays or overcharging by the Mechanic; damage to, or loss, theft or destruction of, the Vehicle or anything in it while it is with the Mechanic or during any test drive; accidents, injury or death arising from the Vehicle or the Repair; the Mechanic closing, disappearing or becoming insolvent; or any claim by a third party connected with the Vehicle or the Repair.

5.2 You will indemnify and hold harmless each Protected Party against all losses, damages, claims, liabilities, penalties and costs (including reasonable legal fees) that arise from: the Repair, or any act or omission of the Mechanic; your instructions to the Mechanic; your use or operation of the Vehicle; any false or inaccurate information or invoice you give; your breach of this Policy; or any claim by a third party relating to the Vehicle.

5.3 Your remedy for a poor repair is against the Mechanic (and any insurer), not against the Protected Parties. Autofixer may, but is not obliged to, help you raise a complaint with the Mechanic.

5.4 Nothing in this Policy excludes or limits liability that cannot be excluded or limited by law, including liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, or death or personal injury caused by a Protected Party’s negligence.

5.5 This clause continues to apply after the loan is repaid.

6. Paying the Lender

6.1 You will repay the Lender as the Loan Agreement says. Repayments, charges, early repayment, hardship arrangements and statements are matters between you and the Lender under the Loan Agreement.

6.2 The Loan Agreement the Lender offers you will state the total amount repayable, each instalment and its due date, the interest rate, any fees, the security and what happens if you default. You are bound only after you accept the Loan Agreement.

6.3 Pay only to the Lender’s designated account(s) shown in the Loan Agreement or in the app. Autofixer does not collect repayments. Never pay instalments into a personal account, and never pay a Mechanic or an Autofixer staff member in cash for instalments. If anyone asks you to, report it.

6.4 Tell us within 7 days if your phone number, address or contact details change, and tell the Lender as your Loan Agreement requires.

6.5 Take repair problems to the Mechanic first, then to Autofixer’s complaints process (clause 9). Unless your Loan Agreement says otherwise, a repair problem does not suspend your repayments.

7. Honest dealing

7.1 You must not: submit a false, altered, duplicated or inflated invoice; agree with a Mechanic to inflate costs, share the money or take cash back; use the financing for anything other than the repair on the invoice; or give someone else’s identity, phone number or Vehicle as your own.

7.2 If we find this has happened, we may refuse or cancel the request, report it to the Lender, the police or regulators, and recover our losses from you. The Lender may treat it as a default under your Loan Agreement.

8. Your information

8.1 You consent to us collecting and using your identity details, phone number, Vehicle details, invoice photo and costs, mechanic details, and information the Lender gives us about your loan (such as default notices and confirmation of repayment), to assess and pass on your request, and prevent fraud.

8.2 We will share this information with the Lender (who uses it to manage the loan, collect repayments and recover what is owed), the Mechanic (only what is needed to confirm the invoice), other members of your group (if you are an Ecoswitch member, as the Membership Terms explain), our technology and communications providers, professional advisers, and regulators or law enforcement when required.

8.3 We handle your data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023 and our Privacy Notice. You may ask to access or correct your data, or object to its use. Withdrawing consent may mean we cannot continue to pass on your request. We keep records for 6 years after your request or loan ends, or longer where the law requires.

8.4 You agree we may contact you by phone, SMS, WhatsApp and email about your request.

9. Complaints and disputes

9.1 Send complaints about Autofixer or the Mechanic to support@autofixer.fund. We will acknowledge within 2 working days and aim to resolve within 14 days. Complaints about the loan itself (charges, repayment, security or recovery) must go to the Lender, using the contact in your Loan Agreement, and we will help pass them on. If you are not satisfied, you may escalate complaints about Autofixer to the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC), and complaints about the loan to the Lender’s own regulator (for example, the CBN where the Lender is CBN-licensed).

9.2 This Policy is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. We will try to settle any dispute by good-faith negotiation for 14 days. If it is not settled, the courts of Lagos State have jurisdiction. This does not limit any right you have by law that cannot be waived.

9.3 Making a complaint to Autofixer does not suspend any repayment due under your Loan Agreement.

10. General

10.1 We may update this Policy. We will give you at least 30 days’ notice.

10.2 You may not transfer your obligations under this Policy.

10.3 Notices sent to the phone number or email you gave us are treated as received 24 hours after sending.

10.4 If any part of this Policy is unenforceable, the rest continues to apply. A delay in enforcing a right is not a waiver of it.

10.5 We keep a record of when and how you accepted this Policy (date, time and device details).

Workshop Terms

To be listed with Autofixer, you agree that for every repair you carry out for an Autofixer customer:

  1. Quality parts. You will buy and use only quality parts for all repairs.
  2. Warranty on the invoice. You will write the repair warranty period on the invoice.
  3. Mediation. If you have a repair dispute with a customer, you will allow Autofixer to mediate. Mediation is not binding: Autofixer will help both sides reach an agreement, but it cannot force either side to accept its suggestion, and nobody gives up the right to go to court.
  4. Commission. Autofixer keeps 10% of the labor cost shown on the invoice as commission. When Autofixer arranges the payment, it is deducted from the payment made to you, so you do not pay it separately. If a customer pays you directly for a repair arranged through Autofixer, you will pay Autofixer 10% of the labor cost within 7 days. It is not added to the customer's bill.

Autofixer may remove you from the list if you break these terms.
