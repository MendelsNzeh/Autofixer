Autofixer Customer Policy: Repair Financing, Repayment and Recovery

1. About this Policy

1.1 This Policy sets out what you agree to, and what we expect of you, when you request or receive financing for a vehicle repair through Autofixer.

1.2 Autofixer is operated by [COMPANY NAME] (RC [NUMBER]) (“Autofixer”). Financing is provided by [LENDER NAME], licensed as [LICENCE DETAILS] (the “Lender”). The Lender alone decides whether to approve a request and on what terms. Autofixer is not a lender.

1.3 If you are approved you will sign a loan agreement with the Lender (the “Loan Agreement”). If this Policy conflicts with the Loan Agreement on the credit terms (amount, charges, repayment schedule or security), the Loan Agreement prevails. In every other respect they apply together.

1.4 By accepting this Policy you confirm that you are at least 18, that you can legally enter this Policy, and that everything you tell us is true and complete.

2. Key terms

  • Vehicle: the vehicle being repaired, identified by the registration number you give us.
  • Mechanic: the mechanic or workshop that carries out the repair.
  • Repair: all work, parts and services supplied for the Vehicle under your request.
  • Financed Amount: the amount the Lender approves to pay for the Repair.
  • Repayment Schedule: the instalments, due dates and total repayable stated in the Loan Agreement.
  • Outstanding Indebtedness: everything you owe the Lender at any time, including the unpaid Financed Amount, interest, fees, default charges and recovery costs.
  • Collateral: the Vehicle and any other asset you expressly give as security in writing.
  • Default: any event listed in clause 9.1.
  • Forced Sale Value: the price a vehicle would reasonably fetch in a sale held within a limited time, as assessed by an independent valuer (clause 10.4).
  • Protected Parties: Autofixer, its affiliates, directors, officers, employees and agents, and the Lender.

3. Who does what

3.1 Autofixer provides the platform you use to submit your request and invoice, passes your request to the Lender, and communicates with you on the Lender’s behalf where agreed. Autofixer does not repair vehicles, inspect repairs or guarantee anyone’s work.

3.2 The Lender assesses requests, funds approved repairs, holds the loan and any security, and collects repayments.

3.3 Mechanics are independent businesses. They are not employees, agents or partners of Autofixer or the Lender. An “approved” mechanic is one that has registered with Autofixer and passed Autofixer’s registration checks. Approval is not a guarantee of skill, quality, price, honesty or outcome.

3.4 You choose your mechanic and you instruct the work. You are the person best placed to check it.

4. Your responsibility for the Repair

4.1 You are responsible for making sure the Repair is done properly. Autofixer and the Lender do not supervise or check the work.

4.2 Before work starts, agree the following with the Mechanic in writing (a message is enough): the faults to be fixed, the work to be done, the parts to be used (new or used, and brand), the price of parts and labor, the time it will take, and any warranty.

4.3 Ask for an itemised invoice. The parts and labor costs you enter on Autofixer must match that invoice exactly.

4.4 Before you accept the Vehicle back, inspect it, take a test drive, ask to see replaced parts, and confirm that all agreed work is done and the Vehicle is safe to drive. If you are unsure, get an independent mechanic to check it.

4.5 Do not sign or confirm completion until you are satisfied. Where the Lender pays the Mechanic after you confirm completion, your confirmation means you accept the work and authorise payment.

4.6 If a defect appears later, tell the Mechanic in writing straight away, allow a reasonable chance to fix it, keep photos and records, and claim under any warranty the Mechanic gave.

4.7 Do not drive a Vehicle you believe to be unsafe.

4.8 Help us with any complaint investigation by providing messages, photos and documents when asked.

4.9 You should keep comprehensive motor insurance on the Vehicle (see clause 8.3).

5. Risk, disclaimers and indemnity

5.1 The Repair is at your risk. The Protected Parties are not responsible for: the quality, safety or completeness of any work; the quality, origin or authenticity of any part; delays or overcharging by the Mechanic; damage to, or loss, theft or destruction of, the Vehicle or anything in it while it is with the Mechanic or during any test drive; accidents, injury or death arising from the Vehicle or the Repair; the Mechanic closing, disappearing or becoming insolvent; or any claim by a third party connected with the Vehicle or the Repair.

5.2 You will indemnify and hold harmless each Protected Party against all losses, damages, claims, liabilities, penalties and costs (including reasonable legal fees) that arise from: the Repair, or any act or omission of the Mechanic; your instructions to the Mechanic; your use or operation of the Vehicle; any false or inaccurate information or invoice you give; your breach of this Policy; or any claim by a third party relating to the Vehicle.

5.3 Your remedy for a poor repair is against the Mechanic (and any insurer), not against the Protected Parties. Autofixer may, but is not obliged to, help you raise a complaint with the Mechanic.

5.4 Nothing in this Policy excludes or limits liability that cannot be excluded or limited by law, including liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, or death or personal injury caused by a Protected Party’s negligence. Subject to that, the Protected Parties’ total liability to you in connection with the Repair is limited to [CAP — lawyer to set].

5.5 This clause continues to apply after the loan is repaid.

6. Repaying what you owe

6.1 You will repay the Outstanding Indebtedness in full, on the due dates in the Repayment Schedule.

6.2 Before you accept any Loan Agreement you will be shown the total amount repayable, each instalment and its due date, the interest rate and any fees. You are bound only after you accept the Loan Agreement.

6.3 You will authorise [direct debit / recurring card payment / bank transfer — confirm with the Lender] and make sure the money is available on each due date.

6.4 Pay only to the Lender’s designated account(s) shown in the Loan Agreement or in the app. Never pay instalments into a personal account, and never pay a Mechanic or an Autofixer staff member in cash for instalments. If anyone asks you to, report it.

6.5 Payments are applied in this order unless the Loan Agreement says otherwise: costs, fees and charges, interest, then principal.

6.6 Late payments attract the charges stated in the Loan Agreement and no others.

6.7 You may repay early, in whole or in part. [Early-repayment terms per the Loan Agreement.]

6.8 Tell us within [7] days if your phone number, address or contact details change, or if the Vehicle is sold, damaged or moved.

6.9 If you expect trouble paying, contact us before the due date. The Lender may, but need not, agree a revised arrangement. Any arrangement must be in writing.

6.10 You can ask at any time for a statement showing what you have paid and what remains.

7. Your repayment does not depend on the Repair

7.1 Your duty to repay is separate from, and independent of, the Repair.

7.2 You must keep paying in full and on time even if: the Repair is late, incomplete, defective or unsafe; the Mechanic refuses to fix it, closes or disappears; the Vehicle is damaged, lost, stolen or written off before or after the Repair; you are in a dispute with the Mechanic; you have made a complaint or asked for a refund; or you disagree with Autofixer or anyone else.

7.3 You will not deduct, set off, counterclaim, withhold, suspend or delay any repayment because of any claim you have, or believe you have, against the Mechanic, Autofixer or anyone else in connection with the Repair.

7.4 Take repair problems to the Mechanic first, then to Autofixer’s complaints process (clause 13), then to the courts or the regulator. These steps run separately and do not pause the Repayment Schedule.

7.5 The one exception is a genuine billing error by the Lender (for example a wrong amount, a duplicate debit or a payment not credited). Tell us in writing, pay any part you do not dispute, and the Lender will investigate and correct it. This exception does not cover any issue with the Repair.

7.6 This clause does not remove any right you have under Nigerian law that cannot be waived.

9. Default

9.1 You are in Default if: (a) you do not pay an instalment in full within [7] days of its due date; (b) you give false or misleading information, or a false, altered or inflated invoice; (c) you break clause 8 or another material term; (d) the Vehicle is sold, transferred, lost, seized or destroyed without the Lender’s consent; (e) you become insolvent or enter an arrangement with creditors; (f) insurance lapses and is not restored within [14] days; or (g) the Lender cannot reach you for [30] days despite reasonable attempts.

9.2 Before taking recovery steps the Lender (or Autofixer for it) will notify you in writing, by SMS, WhatsApp or email, of the Default and give you [14] days to put it right. That period may be shortened where the Collateral is at immediate risk or fraud is suspected.

9.3 If the Default is not put right: the whole Outstanding Indebtedness becomes due immediately; the default charges in the Loan Agreement apply; the Lender may report the Default to licensed credit bureaus; and the Lender may start recovery under clause 10.

9.4 Anyone collecting on the Lender’s behalf must identify themselves and act lawfully and respectfully. They must not threaten or harass you, shame you, or contact your employer, family or phone contacts about the debt except as the law allows.

10. Recovery and sale of the Vehicle

10.1 Handing over. If the Default is not remedied, you must, by the date in the Lender’s notice, hand over the Vehicle with all keys, documents and accessories to the Lender or its authorised agent at the place the notice states, or make it available where it is.

10.2 Cooperation. You must: sign the documents the Lender reasonably needs (transfer, deregistration, consent to sale, insurance claim, registry amendments); attend valuations and inspections; tell us where the Vehicle is; and not hide, damage, strip or move it. Collect your personal belongings before handover; items left behind will be held for [30] days.

10.3 Lawful recovery only. Recovery will be carried out lawfully: without force or breach of the peace, and without entering private premises unless you consent or there is a court order or other lawful authority.

10.4 Valuation and sale. The Lender may sell the Collateral by auction, private sale or another commercially reasonable method after giving you at least [10] days’ written notice of the method, date and place. You agree to take part by allowing viewing and providing information. The Lender will have the Collateral valued by an independent valuer, and you will receive the valuation on request. You agree that a sale at or above the Forced Sale Value, meaning the price reasonably obtainable in a time-limited sale, is an acceptable price. You may bring a buyer who will pay more before the sale date.

10.5 Proceeds. Proceeds are applied in this order: costs of recovery, storage, valuation, repair-for-sale, sale and legal fees; then default charges and interest; then principal.

10.6 Surplus. Anything left after the Outstanding Indebtedness is cleared will be paid to you within [14] days.

10.7 Shortfall. If the proceeds do not cover the Outstanding Indebtedness, you remain liable for the balance and must pay it within [30] days of a written statement. The Lender may use any other lawful means to recover it.

10.8 Right to redeem. At any time before the sale, you may recover the Vehicle by paying the Outstanding Indebtedness in full, including recovery costs.

10.9 Other assets. The Lender may look to assets other than the Collateral only where you have given security over them in writing, or through lawful court process.

10.10 The Lender’s rights under this clause belong to the Lender. Autofixer may coordinate on the Lender’s behalf.

11.1 You must not: submit a false, altered, duplicated or inflated invoice; agree with a Mechanic to inflate costs, share the money or take cash back; use the financing for anything other than the repair on the invoice; or give someone else’s identity, phone number or Vehicle as your own.

11.2 If we find this has happened, we may refuse or cancel the request, treat it as a Default, report it to the Lender, the police or regulators, and recover our losses from you.

12.2 We will share this information with the Lender, licensed credit bureaus, the Mechanic (only what is needed to confirm the invoice), our technology and communications providers, professional advisers, and regulators or law enforcement when required.
