14. Groups

14.1 Why groups. Membership is for people who join a group of members. The members of a group are responsible together for defaults by members of that group, as this section and the lender's group agreement explain. Groups exist to lower the risk of default, and that is why members receive the benefits in clause 3.

14.2 Sign-up and Group ID. After you pay, you must sign up on our website and link your Membership ID to a Group ID. Any member can create a group, which gives them a Group ID. Anyone else joins an existing group as clause 14.13 explains. Your benefits start only when your Membership ID is linked to a Group ID, your group has at least 5 members, and we have approved your Membership (clause 14.16). You should link a group within 30 days of paying. Your 12 months run from the date of payment whether or not you have linked a group. If you have not linked a group within 60 days of paying, you may cancel and receive a full refund.

14.3 Choose carefully. Only join a group with people you know and trust. We do not check whether other members can repay. A group needs at least 5 members before benefits start. There is no maximum size. You can belong to only one group at a time.

14.4 Group responsibility. If a member of your group defaults on a loan made through our lending partner, whether and how the other members of the group are responsible is set only by the lender's group agreement. You will be shown it before you sign it.

14.5 Who you owe. Group responsibility is owed to the lender named in the loan, and is set out in the group agreement that the lender asks you to sign before your first loan is approved. This section alone does not create a debt to the lender or to other members. The group agreement does.

14.6 Order of recovery. The order in which the lender looks to the borrower's own security and to the group is set only by the lender's group agreement.

14.7 Early warning. If a member misses a payment, the lender may tell the other members of the group the member's name and the amount overdue, as the lender's group agreement says.

14.8 Contribution between members. If you pay more than your share of another member's default, you may recover that amount from that member. We will help by sharing the information the law allows.

14.9 Leaving or changing groups. You may not leave your group, or move to another, while you or any member of your group has a loan that is not fully repaid, unless we agree in writing. Cancelling Membership does not end your group responsibility for loans that already exist.

14.10 Removal. We may remove a member from a group, after 14 days’ written notice (or immediately if we suspect fraud), if they misuse the group, give false information, or default repeatedly. Removing someone does not release them from loans they already have.

14.11 Information about members. To run groups, we share with the other members of your group your name and Membership ID and the group's status. Information about missed or defaulted payments is shared by the lender, as clause 14.7 says. Our Privacy Notice explains this.

14.12 Our role. We organise groups and Membership. We do not guarantee any member's loan and we are not responsible for a member's default.

14.13 Joining a group by code. To join an existing group you enter its Group ID. We then email a one-time code to the member who created the group (the "creator"). The creator decides whether to give you the code. When you enter the correct code, you are added to the group automatically. A code expires after 15 minutes and stops working after 5 wrong attempts. Never give a code to anyone you do not know and trust: it lets that person into your group, and makes you responsible, together with the rest of the group, for their defaults.

14.14 The group creator. The creator is a member like any other. The creator's only extra role is that join codes are sent to the creator's email address, so the creator decides who may join. You must keep your email account secure and tell us if you lose access to it. If a creator cannot be reached for 30 days or leaves, the members of the group may choose another member by majority vote to receive join codes, and we will send join codes to that member.

14.15 Groups below 5 members. If a group has fewer than 5 members, for example because a member left or was removed, the benefits of its members pause until it has 5 members again. Loans that already exist continue under their terms, and group responsibility for them continues.

14.16 Approval. Your benefits start only after we have approved your Membership. We review each member to check your identity and payment, and that your phone number and email match before approving, and we may refuse or withdraw approval if we cannot confirm who you are, if information you gave is false, or if we think a group is not safe. If we refuse, we will tell you and refund your fee in full within 10 working days. Approval is decided member by member, so one member's approval does not approve another.

14.17 Email check. When you sign up, we send a one-time code to the email address on your payment. Your Membership is created only when you enter the code. This protects you and your group from people using someone else's payment. You must use the email address you paid with.
