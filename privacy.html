Privacy Notice

Autofixer, including Ecoswitch memberships. Draft: to be reviewed by your lawyer before launch.

1. Who we are

Autofixer (“we”, “us”) runs the Autofixer website, the repair-financing request service, the workshop finder and booking service, and the Ecoswitch membership. We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact us at support@autofixer.fund.

2. What we collect

  • Repair requests: your name, phone number, vehicle registration, the photo of your repair invoice, the parts and labor costs, and the mechanic or workshop details.
  • Workshop bookings: your name, phone number, vehicle registration, a description of what needs fixing, your preferred time, which workshop you booked, and when and from which device you agreed to share these details.
  • How you are paying for a repair: when you send an invoice you tell us whether you are paying by repair loan, cash or bank transfer. For cash or bank transfer we also keep the date you paid and your confirmation that you paid.
  • Ecoswitch memberships: your name, phone number, email, payment reference and amount, Membership ID, group details, and records of when you accepted our terms.
  • Workshops: the workshop name, contact person, phone, email, address and specialties, and records of when and from which device the workshop accepted the Workshop Terms.
  • Technical data: your location, only if you choose “Use my location” to find a workshop, and basic device details (IP address and browser) recorded when you accept a policy, a booking consent or terms.

3. Why we use it

  • To pass your repair request to our lending partner and contact you about it.
  • To run your booking: we share your details with the mechanic you chose, and our team calls you and the mechanic to follow up.
  • To check with the mechanic any payment you tell us you made, and to settle commission owed to us by workshops.
  • To run your membership, take and check payment, and manage groups.
  • To prevent fraud, handle complaints and meet legal duties.

We rely on your consent, on performing our service to you, and on our legal obligations.

4. Who we share it with

  • The lending partner, who uses it to assess the loan, manage it, collect repayments and recover what is owed. The lender has its own privacy notice.
  • The mechanic: what is needed to confirm your invoice, and, when you book a workshop, your name, phone number, vehicle details and what needs fixing. When you book, you also see the mechanic's phone number.
  • Other members of your Ecoswitch group: your name and Membership ID, and group status. The lender may tell them about missed or defaulted payments.
  • Service providers that run our systems: our database and hosting provider, Paystack (payments) and our email provider.
  • Professional advisers, regulators and law enforcement when the law requires.

We do not sell your personal data.

5. Where it is kept and for how long

Your data is stored with our hosting provider in :western Europe. We keep records for 6 years after your request or loan ends, or longer where the law requires.

6. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023 you may ask to access or correct your data, object to its use, ask us to restrict or delete it where the law allows, and withdraw consent. Withdrawing consent may mean we cannot continue your request, booking or membership. Write to support@autofixer.fund. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

7. Changes

We may update this notice and will post the new version here.
